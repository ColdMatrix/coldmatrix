📖 Understanding Your Results

What is BMI?
Body Mass Index is a simple screening tool that estimates body fat using your weight and height. Doctors worldwide use it as a starting point for health conversations — not as a final judgment.
What are BMR and TDEE?
BMR is the energy your body needs at complete rest. TDEE adds your daily activities. Both are gentle estimates to guide you, not strict rules to follow.
Are there limitations?
BMI doesn't know the difference between muscle and fat, or where your body stores energy. Athletes, older adults, and pregnant women may get different readings. Always pair it with how you feel and professional guidance.

📚 Complete Health Guide — Beyond BMI

Dear Friend, BMI is just a starting point — true health is a beautiful daily practice. At ColdMatrix Tools, we believe your body is your home, and it deserves gentle, consistent care — not harsh rules or guilt. Here is a complete, practical guide we wrote with love, especially for our sisters, mothers, daughters, and brothers who want to feel better without pressure.

1. Hydration — Don't Stay Dehydrated 💧
Your body is 60% water. When you are even slightly dehydrated, you feel tired, your head aches, your skin looks dull, and your BMI calculation can even be less accurate. Aim for 2.5 to 3 litres daily for most adults — more if you work in heat or exercise. Keep a 1-litre bottle near you and sip every hour. Add lemon, mint, or a pinch of pink salt for taste and minerals.

2. Nourishing Food — Eat With Love, Not Fear 🍎
Forget extreme diets. Your body needs balance: half plate colorful vegetables and fruits, quarter whole grains, quarter quality protein. Healthy fats like nuts, seeds, ghee in small amounts, and olive oil help your brain and hormones. Eat slowly, chew well, and stop when 80% full.

3. Joyful Movement — Walk, Dance, Breathe 🏃‍♀️
Movement should feel like a gift, not punishment. Start super gently: 20–30 minute walk after dinner with your favorite music. Add 2–3 days of light strength. Dancing in your room counts! Consistency beats intensity every time.

4. Restful Sleep — 7 to 9 Hours is Super Power 😴
Sleep is when your body repairs. Poor sleep raises cortisol, makes you hungrier, and makes weight management harder. Aim for 7–9 hours in a dark, cool room. No screens 30 minutes before bed.

5. Stay Stress-Free — Kindness is Medicine 🌸
Chronic stress raises cortisol, which stores fat around your belly. You deserve peace. Practice 5 minutes of deep breathing daily. Be as kind to yourself as you would be to a dear sister. Small steps, big love — that is the ColdMatrix way.
