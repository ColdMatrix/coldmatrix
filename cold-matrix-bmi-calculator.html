🌿 Your Complete Wellness Picture

BMI is just one number. True health is a beautiful balance of many things.

Nourishment
Enjoy whole foods — colorful fruits, fresh vegetables, wholesome grains, and quality protein. Savor each bite and listen to your body.
Hydration
Sip water gently throughout your day. Herbal teas count too. Your body thrives when well-hydrated.
Joyful Movement
Find activities you love — dancing, walking, yoga, swimming. Movement should feel like a gift, not a punishment.
Rest & Self-Care
Aim for 7–9 hours of restful sleep. Make space for hobbies, nature, and moments of quiet. You deserve it.

📖 Understanding Your Results

What is BMI?
Body Mass Index is a simple screening tool that estimates body fat using your weight and height. Doctors worldwide use it as a starting point for health conversations — not as a final judgment.
What are BMR and TDEE?
BMR is the energy your body needs at complete rest. TDEE adds your daily activities. Both are gentle estimates to guide you, not strict rules to follow.
Are there limitations?
BMI doesn't know the difference between muscle and fat, or where your body stores energy. Athletes, older adults, and pregnant women may get different readings. Always pair it with how you feel and professional guidance.
