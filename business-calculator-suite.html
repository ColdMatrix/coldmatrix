About the Business Calculator Suite

This all-in-one suite brings together the most essential financial calculators for entrepreneurs, accountants, and managers. It now includes 17 powerful calculators covering profit, margin, markup, selling price, discounts, GST/VAT, commission, ROI, break-even, multi-item analysis, simple interest, compound interest, percentage change, cash flow, invoice total, and currency conversion.

Where Each Calculator Is Useful

  • Profit & Margin: Retailers, wholesalers, and manufacturers use these to set pricing and measure profitability.
  • Sell Price & Markup: Essential for quotations and cost-plus pricing strategies.
  • Discount & Reverse Discount: Useful for sales promotions, clearance, and understanding original prices.
  • GST/VAT: Critical for tax invoices in countries with value-added tax systems.
  • Commission: Helps sales teams and agents calculate earnings.
  • ROI: Investors use this to evaluate returns on business or stock investments.
  • Break-even: Essential for startups to know how many units they must sell to cover costs.
  • Multi-Item: For businesses selling multiple products in one invoice.
  • Simple & Compound Interest: Useful for loans, savings, and investment planning.
  • Percentage Change: Tracks growth or decline in sales, revenue, or prices over time.
  • Cash Flow: Monitors money in and out — critical for daily business survival.
  • Invoice Total: Quickly totals line items with tax for billing.
  • Currency Conversion: For import/export and international transactions.

Every calculation runs instantly in your browser, supports multiple currencies, and gives visual summaries so you can quickly see the health of your business. Built for global use — fast, private, and remarkably easy to operate.
