📖 Complete Guide to World Clock & Time Zone Converter

The ColdMatrix World Clock is a powerful, free tool that lets you see live time in any city around the globe and convert time between different time zones instantly. It's designed for international teams, frequent travellers, and anyone who needs to coordinate across regions.

What Is a World Clock?

A world clock displays the current time in multiple cities or time zones simultaneously. Unlike a regular clock that shows only your local time, a world clock lets you monitor the time in several locations at once. The ColdMatrix World Clock goes a step further by adding a time zone converter, so you can input a specific time and see what it corresponds to elsewhere.

Key Features

  • Live Clock Grid: Add any number of cities from a list of over 70 global locations and see their current time, date, and day/night status in real time.
  • Daylight Saving Time (DST) aware: Automatically adjusts for DST changes using your browser's time zone database.
  • Time Zone Converter: Pick a source time zone, a time, and a destination zone to instantly see the converted time and date.
  • Persistent City List: Your selected cities are saved locally in your browser, so they appear every time you visit.
  • Search & Filter: Quickly find a city by typing in the filter box.
  • Day/Night Indicator: Each city card shows whether it is currently daytime or nighttime.
  • Premium Design: Silver and gold themed interface that looks great on any device.
  • Privacy First: All processing is done locally; no data is uploaded to any server.

How to Use the World Clock

  1. View live times: The grid already includes default cities. To add more, click "+ Add City".
  2. Search for a city: Type a name in the filter box to narrow down the dropdown list.
  3. Add city: Select a city from the dropdown and click "Add to My Clocks". The new card will appear instantly.
  4. Remove a city: Click the × button on any clock card to remove it from your list.
  5. Convert a time: In the "Convert a Time" section, choose the source time zone, enter a time (or click "Now"), choose the destination zone, and click "Convert Now".

Tips for Effective Use

  • Keep important cities visible: Add cities where your team members, clients, or family are located.
  • Use for scheduling: Use the converter to find the best meeting time across multiple zones.
  • Plan travel: Check the time difference before booking flights or calling someone abroad.
  • Remember DST: Some regions do not observe daylight saving, but the tool handles this automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool work offline?
Yes, after the initial page load, the clock grid and converter work entirely offline using your browser's time zone data.

Is my city list private?
Absolutely. Your selected cities are stored only in your browser's local storage. No information is sent to any server.

Can I convert between any two time zones?
Yes, the dropdown includes all major cities and UTC. You can convert any time between any two zones.

Why do some cities show the same time?
They may be in the same time zone or currently have the same offset due to DST.

How accurate is the time?
The times are calculated using the Intl API of your browser, which is highly accurate and updates every second.
