About the World Clock & Time Zone Converter

Track live time across multiple cities at once, and convert a specific time from one zone to another — useful for scheduling international calls, coordinating with remote teams, or planning travel. Add any city to your personal clock board, and it stays saved for your next visit.

Common Questions

Does this account for Daylight Saving Time? Yes — times are calculated using your browser's built-in time zone database, which automatically adjusts for daylight saving changes in each region.

Is my selected city list saved? Yes, locally in your own browser only — nothing is sent to a server, so your city list stays private to your device.
