📖 Complete Guide to Electricity Bill Calculator

This calculator helps you estimate your electricity bill based on the units you consume and your tariff rate. It includes fixed charges, taxes, and additional charges for a complete picture.

How Electricity Bills Are Calculated

  • Energy Charges: Units × Tariff Rate.
  • Fixed Charges: Monthly service fee.
  • Tax/GST: Percentage of energy charges.
  • Additional Charges: Meter rent, service fees, etc.

Tips to Reduce Your Bill

  • Use energy-efficient appliances.
  • Turn off unused lights and fans.
  • Use LED bulbs.
  • Avoid peak hours for heavy appliances.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is this calculator accurate?
A: It provides an estimate based on standard tariff structure. Actual bills may vary.

Q: Does it include all taxes?
A: You can enter tax % and additional charges for a complete estimate.
