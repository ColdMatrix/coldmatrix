📚 Complete Guide & Information

About This Tool

ColdMatrix Step Counter & Wellness Dashboard is a mobile-first activity tracking tool that helps you monitor daily walking, distance, calories, hydration, and wellness logs. Built with privacy in mind — all data stays in your browser.

No registration required. No data sent to any server. Complete control over your information.

Why Walking Matters

  • Improved cardiovascular health
  • Better blood sugar regulation
  • Strengthened muscles and bones
  • Enhanced mood and reduced stress
  • Better sleep quality
  • Support for healthy weight management

Walking is one of the simplest and most effective forms of physical activity. Even 30 minutes of daily walking can provide significant health benefits.

How Calculations Work

  • Distance: 0.762 meters per step (average stride length)
  • Calories: ~0.04 kcal per step (general estimate)
  • Speed: Distance ÷ active walking time
  • Pace: Minutes per kilometer

All values are estimates and may vary based on individual factors like height, weight, and walking speed.

Heart Rate & Blood Pressure

These are logging features only. This tool is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis.

How to check pulse manually: Place two fingers on your wrist or neck. Count beats for 15 seconds, then multiply by 4.

For accurate readings, use a fitness band, smartwatch, or dedicated medical device. Consult a healthcare professional for health concerns.

Hydration Guidance

  • Women: About 2.0–2.5 liters per day
  • Men: About 2.5–3.0 liters per day
  • Active individuals: Additional 500ml–1L per hour of exercise

Hydration needs vary based on climate, activity level, and individual factors.

Best Practices for Accuracy

  • Keep phone in pocket or hand while walking
  • Walk naturally — avoid artificial shaking
  • Use manual entry if sensor unavailable
  • Update goals as activity level improves
  • Record heart rate and blood pressure at the same time daily for consistency

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How accurate is the step counter?

Browser-based step counting is approximate. For best results, keep the phone in your pocket. Fitness bands and smartwatches are typically more accurate.

Q: How do I check my heart rate manually?

Place two fingers on your wrist or neck, count beats for 15 seconds, then multiply by 4.

Q: Can this tool measure blood pressure?

No. Blood pressure requires a dedicated device. This tool only logs readings you enter.

Q: How are calories calculated?

Calories are estimated at ~0.04 kcal per step, which is a general average and varies by weight and walking speed.

Q: Is my data safe?

Yes. All data is stored locally in your browser. No registration, no cloud storage.

Q: Can I use this offline?

Yes. Once the page is loaded, all features work offline.

Q: How do I change my goals?

Tap any goal preset or use Custom to set your own target.

Q: Why are my steps different from my fitness band?

Different devices use different sensors and algorithms. Browser-based counting is less precise than dedicated hardware.
