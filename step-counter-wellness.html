Wellness Knowledge Hub
🚶 Why Walking Matters+
Regular walking supports cardiovascular fitness, joint mobility, metabolic rate, and physical wellbeing. Sustained consistency across weeks is far more effective than occasional intense exercise.
💧 Why Hydration Matters+
Hydration assists temperature regulation, physical output, joint lubrication, and replaces moisture lost through physical movement. Fluid needs depend on climate, activity, and diet.
❤️ Understanding Heart Rate+
Tracking resting pulse and post-exercise recovery provides insight into general cardiac efficiency over time. Persistent unusual readings should be reviewed by a medical professional.
🩺 Understanding Blood Pressure+
Systolic and diastolic pressures reflect arterial resistance. Manual logging helps maintain clear personal historical records alongside routine healthcare checkups.
🔥 Understanding Calories+
Active calories reflect energy spent directly through movement, separate from your Resting Metabolic Rate (RMR). Values are estimates calculated from profile and sensor data.
