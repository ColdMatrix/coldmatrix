📚 Complete Guide

ℹ️ About This Tool

The Step Counter & Wellness Dashboard is a free, all-in-one health tracker that runs entirely in your browser. It brings together the five habits that matter most for everyday wellness — movement, heart rate, hydration, blood pressure and sleep — into a single private dashboard. There's no app to install, no account to create, and nothing is ever sent to a server: every reading lives only on your own device, for as long as you choose to keep it.

⚙️ How It Works

Steps: your phone's built-in motion sensor reports tiny accelerations many times per second. Walking produces a distinctive rhythmic pattern in that signal, and the tool detects each stride as a peak above your recent baseline. Keep the phone in your hand or pocket and walk naturally for the best accuracy. No two phones report motion identically, so use Add Manually any time to correct the count.

Heart rate: covering the rear camera lens with a fingertip lets the sensor see the colour of your skin change slightly with every heartbeat, as blood flow rises and falls. The tool watches this signal for 15 seconds and counts the beats to estimate your pulse. Enabling the flash improves the reading, and staying still matters more than pressing hard.

Calories and distance are estimated from your step count using your height and weight, when provided, otherwise typical adult averages are used. Hydration, blood pressure and sleep are simple logs you fill in yourself, so the dashboard can track trends and streaks over time.

🚶 Why Walking Matters

Walking is one of the simplest and most effective forms of physical activity available to almost everyone, regardless of age or fitness level. A regular walking habit is linked to a stronger heart and lungs, better blood sugar control, healthier blood pressure, stronger bones and joints, improved mood, and better sleep quality.

  • Aim for a consistent daily target rather than occasional long walks — 7,000–10,000 steps a day is a widely used general benchmark, but any increase over your current baseline is meaningful.
  • Break walks into shorter sessions if a single long walk isn't practical — three 10-minute walks offer real benefit.
  • Walk at a pace where you can talk but would find it hard to sing, for a good balance of effort and sustainability.
  • Supportive shoes and good posture reduce strain on knees and lower back over time.

💡 Everyday Health Tips

Hydration: a common general guideline is around 2.0–2.5 litres of fluid a day for women and 2.5–3.0 litres for men, with more needed in hot weather, during exercise, or when unwell. Thirst, dark urine colour, and headaches are common early signs of under-hydration.

Sleep: most adults function best on 7–9 hours a night. Consistent sleep and wake times, reduced screen brightness before bed, and a cool, dark room all support better sleep quality.

Heart rate: a normal resting heart rate for most adults falls between 60–100 beats per minute, and tends to be lower in people who exercise regularly. Rate can rise from caffeine, stress, recent activity, or measuring incorrectly — measure while calm and seated for the most consistent readings.

Blood pressure: a commonly cited normal range is under 120/80 mmHg. Readings consistently at or above 130/80 mmHg, or any very high or very low reading accompanied by symptoms, are worth discussing with a healthcare professional rather than managing from a log alone.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Is my data private?

Yes. Every reading — steps, heart rate, hydration, blood pressure and sleep — is stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to any server, and you can delete it at any time from the Privacy & Data section.

How accurate is the step counter?

It uses your phone's motion sensor and a walking-pattern detection algorithm, similar in principle to dedicated pedometer apps. Accuracy depends on your device's sensor quality and how you carry your phone. It is a wellness estimate, not a clinical-grade measurement — use Add Manually to correct the count any time.

Is the heart rate reading medically accurate?

No — it's a camera-based wellness estimate intended for general awareness, not a medical device. It should never be used to diagnose, monitor, or manage a heart condition. If you have concerns about your heart rate, speak with a healthcare professional or use a certified medical device.

Why does the heart rate measurement need camera access?

The camera is the only sensor most phones have that can detect the subtle colour change in your fingertip caused by blood flow with each heartbeat. Your browser will ask for camera permission the first time — the video feed is processed instantly on your device and is never recorded or transmitted.

Does this work without an internet connection?

Yes, once the page has loaded, all tracking, calculations and storage happen locally on your device and continue to work offline.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, it's completely free with no sign-up required, as part of ColdMatrix's collection of free daily-use tools.
