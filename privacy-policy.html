Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 31, 2026  |  Last Updated: August 31, 2026

At ColdMatrix Tools, accessible from https://coldmatrixtools.com, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights under applicable data protection laws.

1. Introduction

This policy applies only to our online activities and is valid for visitors to our website regarding information shared or collected on ColdMatrix Tools. It does not apply to any information collected offline or through other channels.

By using ColdMatrix Tools, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide and improve our services. The types of data we may collect include:

  • Direct Communications: If you contact us via email or contact forms, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and the contents of your message.
  • Usage Data: When you use our calculators and tools, we may collect technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring URLs.
  • Cookies and LocalStorage: We use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences (e.g., metric/imperial units, language) and to enhance your browsing experience.
  • Analytics Data: We use Google Analytics to understand traffic patterns and improve user experience. This data is aggregated and anonymized.

🔐 Important: We do not store your calculation inputs or results on our servers. All calculator operations are performed locally in your browser unless you explicitly save them.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our tools and services.
  • To understand how visitors use our website and diagnose technical issues.
  • To display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense.
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.
  • To comply with legal obligations.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, our legal basis for collecting and using your personal information depends on the data concerned and the specific context. We process your data on the following legal grounds:

  • Consent: You have given us permission to process your data for a specific purpose (e.g., cookies, advertising preferences).
  • Legitimate Interests: Processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests, such as improving our website, ensuring security, and preventing fraud.
  • Legal Obligation: Processing is necessary to comply with applicable laws.
  • Contract: Processing is necessary to fulfil a contract with you.

5. Log Files

ColdMatrix Tools follows standard log file procedures. These files log visitors when they access the website and may include IP addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click patterns. This data is used for analytics, security, and performance monitoring and is not linked to personally identifiable information.

6. Cookies and Web Beacons

We use cookies to store your preferences and recognize you on future visits. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, but some features may not function properly.

Types of cookies we use:

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the website to function (e.g., remembering your preferences).
  • Analytics Cookies: Google Analytics cookies that help us understand how visitors use our site.
  • Advertising Cookies: Google AdSense cookies that help display relevant ads.
  • LocalStorage: Used by our free tools to save your inputs and settings locally on your device. This data never leaves your browser.
Google AdSense and Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DART cookie) to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other websites.

You may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google Ads Settings page.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks may use cookies, JavaScript, or web beacons to measure ad effectiveness and personalize content. ColdMatrix Tools has no access to or control over these third-party cookies.

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit: Google's Partner Sites Policy.

7. Third-Party Privacy Policies

ColdMatrix Tools's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. We advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of any third-party ad servers for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions on how to opt out of certain options.

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. For more information about cookie management with specific web browsers, visit the browsers' respective websites.

8. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right to Access: Request copies of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data under certain conditions.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request restriction of processing under certain conditions.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request transfer of your data to another organization or directly to you.
  • Right to Object: Object to processing of your data under certain conditions.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw any consent you have given at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below. We will respond within 30 days.

Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority in your country of residence if you believe our processing of your personal data violates applicable law.

9. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the CCPA:

  • Right to Know: Know what personal information we collect, use, or disclose.
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt-out of the sale of your personal information (we do not sell personal data).
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.

To submit a request, contact us at the email below. We will verify your request before processing.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

  • Contact Form Submissions: Retained for as long as needed to respond, then archived for record-keeping.
  • Analytics Data: Retained for up to 26 months, per Google Analytics standards.
  • LocalStorage in Tools: Stored only on your device, and only until you clear your browser data.

11. International Data Transfers

ColdMatrix Tools is based in Pakistan. Our service providers (such as Google) may process your data in countries outside your own. Where required by law, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

12. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously. We use industry-standard measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us, and we will promptly delete it.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately.

14. Do Not Track Signals

Our website does not respond to "Do Not Track" (DNT) browser signals. However, you can manage tracking through cookie settings, browser settings, and Google's Ad Settings.

15. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of these sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any third-party site you visit.

16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
