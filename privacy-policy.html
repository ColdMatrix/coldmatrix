Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At ColdMatrix Tools, accessible from https://coldmatrixtools.com, the privacy of our visitors is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by ColdMatrix Tools and how we use it.

This Privacy Policy applies only to our online activities and is valid for visitors to our website with regards to the information that they shared and/or collect in ColdMatrix Tools. This policy is not applicable to any information collected offline or via channels other than this website.

Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

Information We Collect

We collect different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our service to you.

Types of Data Collected

  • Usage Data — We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage data including pages visited, time spent on pages, approximate geographic location (country and city level), device type, browser type, and referring website.
  • Contact Data — If you contact us directly via our contact page, WhatsApp, or our lead capture forms, we may receive additional information about you such as your name, email address, WhatsApp number, city, country, and the contents of your message.
  • Lead Capture Data — When you use our "Download Certified Technical Estimate" feature, we collect your Full Name, Business Email, WhatsApp number, City, and Country so we can send you the requested PDF report and relevant project information. This data is processed by third‑party services such as Formspree (or similar) to deliver the lead to our business email.
  • Calculator Data — Any values you enter into our calculators are processed locally in your web browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit these values to any server.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our website
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our website
  • Understand and analyze how you use our website
  • Develop new products, services, features, and functionality
  • Communicate with you, either directly or through one of our partners, including for customer service and support
  • Send you updates, quotes, and other information relating to the website, if you have requested such communications
  • Find and prevent fraud
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide quotes when requested

Log Files

ColdMatrix Tools follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. All hosting companies do this and it is part of hosting services' analytics. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement on the website, and gathering demographic information.

How Calculator Data Is Handled

One of the most important features of ColdMatrix Tools is privacy-friendly calculators. All calculations, including:

  • Cold room and sandwich panel calculations
  • Steel weight and PEB material estimations
  • Cooling requirement (BTU/TR) calculations
  • Business, ROI, and startup cost calculations
  • BMI, loan EMI, and currency conversions
  • Women's Corner clothing, fabric, beauty, and jewelry calculations

...are performed entirely in your browser using JavaScript. No data entered into any calculator is sent to our servers, stored in any database, or accessible to us. This ensures complete privacy for your calculations.

Like any other website, ColdMatrix Tools uses cookies. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
