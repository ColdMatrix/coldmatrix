Cold Storage Construction Cost in Pakistan (2026) — Full Breakdown & Calculator

Cold storage is booming in Pakistan, driven by the food processing, pharmaceutical, and export sectors. Whether you're planning a small walk-in freezer for a restaurant or a large industrial cold room, understanding the cost is critical. In this guide, we break down every component that affects cold storage construction cost in Pakistan — and show you how to estimate your project accurately.

1. Key Factors Affecting Cold Store Cost

The total cost of a cold store in Pakistan depends on several variables:

  • Room dimensions – larger rooms need more panels and higher refrigeration capacity.
  • Temperature requirements – deep freezers (-25°C) cost more than chillers (+4°C) due to thicker insulation and more powerful refrigeration.
  • Panel type and thickness – PUF (polyurethane foam) is standard; PIR (polyisocyanurate) is used for fire-rated or premium projects. Typical thickness: 100mm for chillers, 150mm for freezers.
  • Refrigeration system – monoblock vs split vs rack systems; capacity measured in HP/TR.
  • Door type, floor insulation, and installation complexity – these add 10-20% to the total.

3. Panel Cost: PUF vs PIR

  • PUF is the most common choice for cold rooms in Pakistan due to its excellent insulation and lower cost. Standard density is 40–45 kg/m³.
  • PIR costs 15–20% more but offers better fire resistance and is required in many export-oriented or pharmaceutical facilities.

For most Pakistani commercial projects, 100mm PUF is sufficient for chillers and 150mm PUF for freezers. In hot regions like Sindh and Punjab, consider one step thicker for energy savings.

4. Refrigeration Costs

Refrigeration is the second largest cost after panels. The size (TR) is calculated based on heat load (walls, product, infiltration, and equipment). A rough guide:

  • 1 TR cools approximately 300–500 ft³ of chiller space.
  • 1 TR cools approximately 200–300 ft³ of freezer space.

For a 1,500 ft² chiller (12 ft height), you might need 8–10 TR. At Rs. 400,000 per TR, that's Rs. 3.2–4 million just for the refrigeration package.

5. Hidden Costs to Watch

  • Vapour barrier and sealing – essential for preventing ice formation.
  • Electrical work – separate power supply, stabilizers, wiring.
  • Freight and handling – if panels are not locally sourced.
  • Government approvals – especially for commercial projects.

Always add a 10–15% contingency to your budget.

FAQ

Q: Can I build a cold store myself?

A: For small rooms (<100 ft²) you might manage, but for commercial projects always hire professionals. Improper installation voids panel warranties and reduces efficiency.

Q: How long does it take to build a cold store in Pakistan?

A: Typically 2–4 weeks for panel manufacturing and 1–2 weeks for installation, depending on size.

Q: What is the lifespan of a cold store?

A: With proper maintenance, 15–25 years.
