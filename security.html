Security & Privacy

Last Updated: August 31, 2026

At ColdMatrix Tools, your security and privacy are our highest priority. We believe you should be able to use professional tools without worrying about your data being collected, tracked, or sold. Our platform is designed with privacy-first principles from the ground up.

🔐
SSL Secured
HTTPS encryption protects your connection
🚫
No Data Collection
Your inputs are never seen or stored
📱
Local Processing

✅
No Registration
Use every tool without creating an account

🛡️ How We Protect You

  • SSL Encryption: Your connection to ColdMatrix is encrypted using HTTPS. This prevents third parties from intercepting your data.
  • No Server Storage: Every tool runs 100% in your browser. Your inputs, calculations, and results never leave your device.
  • No Registration Required: You can use every tool without creating an account, providing an email, or sharing personal information.
  • No Tracking Cookies: We use minimal cookies only for essential functions. We don't track your browsing behavior or sell your data.

🚫 What We Don't Do

  • We don't collect names, emails, or phone numbers unless you contact us voluntarily
  • We don't sell or share any data with third parties
  • We don't use invasive advertising or trackers
  • We don't require downloads or installations
  • We don't access your camera, microphone, or files without explicit permission

🎛️ Your Control

  • Clear local data anytime from your browser settings
  • Use incognito mode for complete isolation
  • Delete saved records from tool settings
  • Contact us with any privacy concerns

📊 How We Handle Data

  • Calculator Inputs: Never sent to our servers. Everything stays in your browser.
  • LocalStorage: Some tools save your preferences locally on your device only (like notepad text, business planner entries, favorite tools). This data never leaves your device and can be cleared anytime from your browser.
  • Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand general traffic patterns — anonymized and aggregated, never linked to you personally.
  • Contact Forms: If you reach out to us voluntarily, we use your info only to respond to your inquiry.

🔎 Found a security issue? If you discover any vulnerability on our website, we'd love to hear from you. Please contact us at contact@coldmatrixtools.com and we'll address it promptly.

❓ Questions?

If you have any security or privacy questions, we're here to help. Reach out through our contact page or review our detailed privacy policy.
