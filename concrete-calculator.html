About the Concrete Calculator

Calculate concrete volume, cement bags, sand, and aggregate needed for slabs, footings, columns, and beams. Built for contractors, site engineers, and homeowners planning construction — enter your dimensions and mix ratio to get material quantities in seconds.

Common Questions

What concrete mix ratio should I use? Common ratios are 1:2:4 (cement:sand:aggregate) for general slabs and footings, and 1:1.5:3 for structural elements needing higher strength — this tool lets you select or customize the ratio.
