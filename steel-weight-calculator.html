About the Steel Weight Calculator

Quickly calculate the weight and estimated cost of structural steel — angles, channels, beams, pipes, and plates — commonly used in PEB steel structures, industrial sheds, and prefab construction. Built for engineers, fabricators, and contractors who need a fast on-site or in-office weight estimate without digging through steel tables.

Common Questions

How is structural steel weight calculated? Steel weight is based on the shape's cross-sectional area, length, and steel density (typically 7850 kg/m³). This tool applies standard formulas for each shape so you get an instant, reliable estimate.

Is this useful for PEB structures? Yes — pair it with our PEB Material Calculator for a complete structure estimate, or the Cold Store Calculator if you're building insulated cold storage.
