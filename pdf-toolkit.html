📖 Complete PDF Toolkit Guide

ColdMatrix PDF Toolkit is your all-in-one PDF management solution. With 7 powerful tools in one place, you can merge, split, compress, rotate, delete pages, and convert between PDF, JPG, PNG, and Word formats — with speed and precision. Everything runs privately in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Complete Feature List

  • 🔗 Merge PDFs: Combine multiple PDF files into a single document. Perfect for combining reports, invoices, or chapters into one file.
  • ✂️ Split PDF: Extract specific pages or split every page into separate files. Ideal for sharing only relevant sections of large documents.
  • 🗜️ Compress PDF: Reduce file size for easy email sharing. Choose from three compression levels to balance quality and size.
  • 🔄 Rotate PDF: Fix incorrectly oriented pages with 90°, 180°, or 270° rotation. Great for scanned documents.
  • 🗑️ Delete Pages: Remove unwanted pages from a PDF. Useful for cleaning up documents before sharing.
  • 🖼️ JPG/PNG ⇄ PDF: Convert images to PDF or extract PDF pages as images. Perfect for archiving photos or creating image-based documents.
  • 📝 PDF ⇄ Word: Extract text from PDFs for editing, or convert Word documents to PDF format.

How Each Tool Works

🔗 Merge: Select two or more PDF files. The tool combines all pages from each file, in the order you selected them, into a single new PDF document.

✂️ Split: Enter a page range like 1-3,5 to extract only those pages into a new PDF. Leave the field blank to split every page into its own separate PDF (delivered as a ZIP file).

🗜️ Compress: Choose a compression level and the tool rebuilds your PDF from flattened page images at a lower quality — dramatically reducing file size for email sharing.

🔄 Rotate: Pick an angle (90°, 180°, or 270°) and every page in your PDF is rotated to fix orientation issues from scans or phone photos.

🗑️ Delete Pages: Enter the pages you want removed (like 1,3,5 or 1-3) and the tool produces a new PDF with just the pages you want to keep.

🖼️ Image ⇄ PDF: Convert one or more JPG/PNG images into a single PDF (each image becomes a page), or extract every page of a PDF as a JPG or PNG.

📝 PDF ⇄ Word: Extract the text from any PDF into an editable Word file, or convert a .docx Word document into a clean PDF.

Why PDF Format Matters

PDF (Portable Document Format) is the universal standard for document sharing. It preserves formatting, fonts, images, and layout across all devices — a PDF created on Windows looks identical on Mac, Android, iPhone, or any other platform. This is why PDF is the preferred format for contracts, resumes, invoices, and official documents. Our toolkit gives you all the essential PDF operations you need in one free, private, browser-based suite.

Common Questions

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?
No — remove the password first, then merge.

Does compression reduce quality?
Yes, higher compression may slightly reduce quality. Use medium for the best balance.

Is there a file size limit?
No hard limit, but very large files may take longer to process depending on your device.

Can I use this on mobile?
Yes, fully responsive and works on all devices including smartphones and tablets.

Are my files uploaded to a server?
No. Every tool runs 100% locally in your browser. Your documents never leave your device.

What PDF operations are supported?
Merge, split, compress, rotate, delete pages, image conversion, and Word conversion.

Does this work offline?
Yes — after the first load, the toolkit works entirely without internet.
