About the PDF Toolkit

ColdMatrix PDF Toolkit gives you everything you need to manage PDF files directly in your browser — no uploads, no sign‑ups, and no privacy worries. Merge multiple PDFs into one, split large documents into separate pages, reduce file size for easy sharing, and convert between PDF, images, and Word formats with just a few taps.

Whether you're preparing client proposals, scanning documents, or compressing reports for email, this toolkit is fast, private, and works entirely on your device. Your files never leave your computer or phone, making it safe for sensitive business and personal documents.
