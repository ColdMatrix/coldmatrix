About the Age Calculator

ColdMatrix Premium Age Calculator gives you your exact age in years, months, days, hours, and minutes — plus insightful extras like your zodiac sign, Chinese zodiac, day of birth, and days remaining until your next birthday. The interface adapts elegantly to your selected gender, with a luxurious dark/gold theme for men and a beautiful soft pink/purple sparkle theme for women.

Simply enter your name, gender, and date of birth, tap Calculate, and you'll receive a complete, beautifully presented age report that you can share on WhatsApp or download as an image or PDF. Everything runs privately in your browser — no sign‑up, no tracking, no storage.
