What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, mobile phone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to website owners about how visitors interact with the site.

Cookies can be "persistent" or "session" cookies:

  • Session Cookies — Temporary cookies that are deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent Cookies — Remain on your device until they expire or you delete them manually.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • To understand how visitors use our website

  • To track which tools are most popular among users
  • To analyze traffic patterns and visitor behavior
  • To store user preferences for future visits

Types of Cookies We Use

1. Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable basic features like page navigation and access to secure areas of the website. The website cannot function properly without these cookies.

2. Analytics Cookies

We use Google Analytics to help us understand how visitors engage with our website. These cookies collect information such as:

  • Number of visitors to the website
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Approximate geographic location (country/city level)

This information is anonymized and aggregated. We cannot identify you personally from this data.

3. Preference Cookies

These cookies allow the website to remember choices you make, such as your preferred currency, language, or region. They provide a more personalized experience.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may also use cookies provided by trusted third parties. For example:

Your Choices Regarding Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences in the following ways:

Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • Delete cookies from your browser
  • Block cookies from being set
  • Set alerts before cookies are placed
  • Use incognito/private browsing mode

Google Analytics Opt-Out

You can opt-out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Visit: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

Ad Settings

You can manage your ad preferences through Google Ad Settings at: https://adssettings.google.com

Important Note

Disabling cookies will not affect the functionality of our calculators. All calculator operations work entirely in your browser and do not require cookies.

Cookies and Personal Data

Cookies themselves do not contain personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. However, they may be linked to other data held by third-party services like Google Analytics.
