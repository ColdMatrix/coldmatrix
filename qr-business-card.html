📖 About the QR Business Card Generator

Design a professional digital business card with a built-in scannable QR code — share your contact details instantly with anyone, no app required on their end. This tool creates a complete vCard (virtual contact card) that contains your name, job title, company, phone, email, website, and address in a single scannable code.

Complete Feature List

  • Digital Business Card: Create a complete vCard with name, title, company, phone, email, website, and address.
  • QR Code Generation: Automatically generates a scannable QR code containing all your contact details.
  • Download & Share: Export as PDF, JPG, or PNG, and share on WhatsApp.
  • Print-Ready: Use on printed cards, email signatures, or digital profiles.
  • Universal Compatibility: Works with all modern smartphone cameras — no special app needed.
  • 100% Free: No registration, no hidden fees, no watermarks.

Where QR Business Cards Are Used

  • Networking Events: Share your contact instantly without physical cards.
  • Email Signatures: Add a QR code to your email footer for one-tap contact saving.
  • Printed Cards: Include QR on traditional business cards for quick saving.
  • Social Media Profiles: Link your digital card in Instagram, LinkedIn, or Twitter bios.
  • Conferences & Trade Shows: Quick contact exchange with potential clients and partners.
  • Freelancers & Consultants: Share your professional details with clients effortlessly.

How It Works

  1. Enter Your Details: Fill in your name, title, company, phone, email, website, and address.
  2. Generate: Click the "Generate My QR Business Card" button.
  3. Preview: See your digital business card with the QR code instantly.
  4. Share or Download: Share on WhatsApp or download as PDF, JPG, or PNG.
  5. Scan to Save: Anyone who scans your QR code can save your contact directly to their phone.

Tips for an Effective Digital Business Card

  • Use your full professional name: Avoid nicknames unless you're in a creative industry.
  • Include a clear job title: This helps people understand what you do.
  • Double-check your phone and email: These are the most important fields — verify them carefully.
  • Add your website: A professional website or LinkedIn profile adds credibility.
  • Keep it updated: Regenerate your QR code if your contact details change.

Common Questions

How is this different from the QR Code Generator?
This tool is purpose-built for business cards specifically, with layout and design options. The QR Code Generator is for general links, text, or WiFi codes.

Can I use this on my printed business card?
Yes! Download the QR code and add it to your print design. The QR code works perfectly in print as long as it's at least 2cm × 2cm.

Does it work with all phones?
Yes, any modern smartphone camera can scan the QR code and save the contact — both iPhone and Android.

Can I edit my card after generating?
Yes, simply change the fields and click "Generate" again to update the QR code.

Is there a limit on how many cards I can create?
No limit — create as many cards as you need for different roles or businesses.

Disclaimer: This tool creates standard vCard format QR codes. Always test the QR code with your own phone before printing large quantities.
