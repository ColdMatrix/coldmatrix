Why PDF Format Matters

PDF (Portable Document Format) is the universal standard for document sharing because it preserves formatting, fonts, images, and layout across all devices and operating systems. A PDF created on Windows looks identical on Mac, Android, iPhone, or any other platform. This makes PDF the preferred format for contracts, resumes, invoices, and official documents.

Common Questions

Can I convert a Word document to PDF?
Yes, simply switch to the "Convert to PDF" tab and select your .doc or .docx file.

Can I convert an image to PDF?
Yes, the converter supports JPG, PNG, GIF, BMP, and WebP formats.

Does it work on mobile?
Yes, fully responsive and works on all devices including smartphones and tablets.

Can I read password-protected PDFs?
Basic password protection may work if the password is entered. Complex encryption may require desktop software.

Is there a file size limit?
Large files may take longer to process. For very large PDFs, we recommend compressing them first.

Tips for Best Results

  • Use high-quality images: When converting images to PDF, ensure the source image is clear and high resolution.
  • Check page orientation: For documents, make sure the content fits properly in portrait or landscape mode.
  • Keep original files: Always maintain backups of your original documents before conversion.
  • Use zoom for small text: The zoom controls help you read fine print comfortably.
