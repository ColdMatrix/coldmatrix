Format Comparison Guide

  • JPG: Best for photos and complex images. Small file size, no transparency.
  • PNG: Best for graphics, logos, and images with transparency. Larger file size.
  • WebP: Modern format with superior compression. Smaller than both JPG and PNG.
  • PDF: Best for documents and sharing images as a single file.
