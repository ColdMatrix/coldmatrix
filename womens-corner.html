🌸 Complete Guide to Women's Corner

Welcome to the Women's Corner — a beautiful collection of four practical calculators designed for women around the world. This multi-tool helps you calculate clothing costs, fabric requirements, beauty budgets, and jewelry values, so you can make informed decisions about your everyday and special purchases.

👗 1. Clothing & Fabric Cost Calculator

This tool helps you calculate the true cost of making or buying clothing. Simply enter the fabric price, quantity needed, and all accessories like lining, buttons, zippers, and embroidery. It adds everything together, including stitching charges, to show you the total garment cost.

Whether you're getting a dress made or planning a wardrobe, this calculator gives you a clear picture of expenses. When ordering from a tailor, always ask for a breakdown of:

  • Fabric cost — varies by quality (cotton, silk, lawn, chiffon, georgette, etc.)
  • Accessories cost — lining, buttons, zippers, lace, embroidery, beads
  • Stitching charges — usually 15–30% of total cost
  • Extra work — hand embroidery, designer cuts, custom fitting

🧵 2. Fabric Quantity Calculator

Not sure how much fabric you need? This calculator estimates the required fabric based on the garment type, fabric width, and garment length. It supports inches, centimeters, meters, and yards, making it useful for tailors, designers, and home sewers around the world.

Why fabric width matters: A wider fabric (60 inches) requires less yardage than a narrower one (36 inches) for the same garment because you can fit more pattern pieces side by side. Our calculator now adjusts the estimate based on your chosen width.

GarmentTypical Fabric (45" width)
Simple Shirt2.5 – 3 yards
Dress (Knee-length)3 – 4 yards
Formal Suit4 – 5 yards
Abaya (Simple)3 – 4 yards
Trousers1.5 – 2 yards
Skirt (Long)2 – 3 yards

Important: Always add 10–15% extra fabric for safety — for pattern matching on prints, direction of the fabric, and cutting errors. Our calculator adds a small margin automatically.

💄 3. Beauty & Makeup Budget Calculator

Plan your beauty expenses with this simple budget calculator. Enter your monthly spending on makeup, skincare, haircare, salon visits, nails, and fragrance. The tool calculates your monthly and annual beauty costs, helping you manage your personal care budget effectively.

Smart budgeting tips:

  • Invest in skincare — a good foundation beats a fancy lipstick
  • Buy multi-purpose products — one highlighter can double as an eyeshadow
  • Track your annual spend — most people underestimate by 30%
  • Rotate products — don't hoard; use what you buy
  • Salon visits — spacing them 4–6 weeks is healthier and cheaper

💎 4. Jewelry Weight & Price Calculator

Calculate the estimated value of gold, silver, or platinum jewelry. Enter the weight, metal type, price per gram, making charges, wastage percentage, and stone charges. The tool computes the metal value, making charges, wastage, and final estimated jewelry price.

Understanding the components:

  • Metal value = weight × price per gram (per today's rate)
  • Making charges = 8–15% of metal value (labor)
  • Wastage = 3–8% (metal lost during crafting)
  • Stone charges = variable, based on gemstone type
  • Hallmarking / GST = additional tax in most countries

Gold weight units commonly used:

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are the fabric requirements accurate for tailoring?
A: They're based on standard patterns. Always confirm with your tailor for complex designs or patterned fabric.

Q: Which gold weight unit should I use?
A: Use Tola in Pakistan, India, and Bangladesh. Use grams internationally. Use ounces if buying from US or UK dealers.

Q: Do jewelry prices include taxes?
A: No — the calculator shows the pre-tax estimated price. Add GST/VAT as applicable in your country.

Q: Can I use this for business?
A: Yes! Boutiques, tailors, beauty salons, and jewelry shops commonly use it for quick quotes.
