🌸 Complete Guide to Women's Corner

Welcome to the Women's Corner — a beautiful collection of four practical calculators designed for women around the world. This multi-tool helps you calculate clothing costs, fabric requirements, beauty budgets, and jewelry values, so you can make informed decisions about your everyday and special purchases.

👗 1. Clothing & Fabric Cost Calculator

This tool helps you calculate the true cost of making or buying clothing. Simply enter the fabric price, quantity needed, and all accessories like lining, buttons, zippers, and embroidery. It adds everything together, including stitching charges, to show you the total garment cost. Whether you're getting a dress made or planning a wardrobe, this calculator gives you a clear picture of expenses.

🧵 2. Fabric Quantity Calculator

Not sure how much fabric you need? This calculator estimates the required fabric based on the garment type, fabric width, and garment length. It supports inches, centimeters, meters, and yards, making it useful for tailors, designers, and home sewers around the world. Remember, this is an estimate — actual fabric consumption depends on the design and cutting pattern.

💄 3. Beauty & Makeup Budget Calculator

Plan your beauty expenses with this simple budget calculator. Enter your monthly spending on makeup, skincare, haircare, salon visits, nails, and fragrance. The tool calculates your monthly and annual beauty costs, helping you manage your personal care budget effectively.

💎 4. Jewelry Weight & Price Calculator

Calculate the estimated value of gold, silver, or platinum jewelry. Enter the weight, metal type, price per gram, making charges, wastage percentage, and stone charges. The tool computes the metal value, making charges, wastage, and final estimated jewelry price. This is especially useful when buying or selling jewelry to ensure you're paying a fair price.

Why Use Women's Corner?

  • Save Money: Know the true cost before making purchases.
  • Plan Better: Calculate fabric needs and beauty budgets with confidence.
  • Global Support: Multiple currencies and units for worldwide use.
  • Beautiful Interface: Soft colors and elegant design make it a joy to use.
  • Private & Free: No registration, no data storage.

Important Disclaimer

Fabric requirements are estimates and can vary based on design, cutting pattern, fabric width, and garment construction. Jewelry pricing varies according to market rates, workmanship, and seller charges. Always confirm with your tailor or jeweler for exact figures.
