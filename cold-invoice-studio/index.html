📘 About the ColdMatrix Invoice Studio

Create professional invoices, quotations, estimates, and business proposals with executive-grade paper templates — export as PDF, PNG, or JPG, and share directly via WhatsApp. Built for contractors, consultants, freelancers, and business owners who need polished business documents fast.

What You Can Create

  • Invoice: Bill clients for completed work with payment tracking — Paid amount, Balance due, due date, and PO number.
  • Quotation: Send professional price quotes before work begins, so clients can approve in writing.
  • Estimate: Preliminary cost projections for larger projects where final numbers aren't set yet.
  • Proposal: Full business proposals combining scope, pricing, and terms in one document.

What's Inside Every Document

  • Your business logo, name, tagline, address, phone, email, and GST/Tax ID
  • Client name, company, and address
  • Document number, date, due date, and PO number
  • Itemized list with description, quantity, rate, and amount
  • Automatic subtotal, tax, shipping, discount, and balance due
  • Multi-currency support: PKR, USD, INR, EUR, GBP, AED, SAR

Why Businesses Trust This Tool

  • No signup required — open the page and start creating instantly
  • Multi-currency — invoice clients in their local currency
  • Real image export — send the actual invoice as an image on WhatsApp
  • Save Draft — your data stays in your browser, safe and private
  • Three templates — Minimal, Gold Pro, and Dark Executive for every business style

Best Practices for Professional Invoices

  • Number sequentially: INV-2026-001, INV-2026-002 — makes record-keeping easy.
  • Always add a due date: 7 to 30 days is standard for most businesses.
  • Itemize clearly: Vague line items delay payments. Be specific.
  • Include tax ID: Required for business-to-business transactions in most countries.
  • Follow up politely: Send a gentle reminder 2 days before the due date.
  • Keep a copy: Download and archive every invoice you send.
