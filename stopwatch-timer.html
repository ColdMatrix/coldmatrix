About the Stopwatch & Timer

ColdMatrix Stopwatch & Timer is a precision chronograph designed for sports, cooking, workouts, presentations, and classroom activities. It offers lap tracking with millisecond accuracy and a countdown timer that keeps counting even if you switch tabs or lock your phone.

Everything runs privately in your browser — no data is sent anywhere. The tool is completely free, works offline after the first visit, and requires no registration. Whether you're timing laps, baking, or managing a presentation, it’s fast, accurate, and always ready.
