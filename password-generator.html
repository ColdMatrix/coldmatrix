About the Password Generator

Create strong, unpredictable passwords for any account — email, banking, social media, or work logins. Customize the length and which character types to include, and generate as many new passwords as you need, instantly, right in your browser.

Common Questions

Is my password sent anywhere or stored? No — every password is generated locally on your own device using your browser's cryptographically secure random number generator. Nothing is ever transmitted or saved.

What makes a password strong? Length matters more than complexity — a longer password (12+ characters) with a mix of character types is far harder to crack than a short, complex one. Aim for at least 14 characters where the account allows it.

Should I reuse passwords across sites? No — using a unique password for every account means a breach on one site can't be used to access your others. Consider a password manager to keep track of them all.
