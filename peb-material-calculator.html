What Is a PEB Structure?

A Pre-Engineered Building (PEB) is a steel structure that is designed, fabricated, and assembled using standardized sections and components. Unlike conventional buildings that are built piece by piece on site, PEB structures are pre-designed in a factory, then delivered and assembled quickly. They are widely used for warehouses, factories, cold storage, showrooms, and commercial buildings because they offer faster construction, lower cost, and high strength.

How the PEB Material Calculator Works

This calculator estimates the major material quantities for a PEB based on building dimensions, bay spacing, crane load, and insulation type. It uses typical industry factors to approximate steel weight and sheeting area. While it gives a reliable preliminary estimate, final structural design must be done by a licensed engineer.

Key Inputs Explained

  • Length & Width: Determine the building footprint and roof area.
  • Eave Height: Affects wall area and steel column weight.
  • Bay Spacing: Smaller spacing means more frames but lighter members; larger spacing means fewer frames but heavier sections.
  • Crane Load: Adds significant steel for crane girders and stronger columns.
  • Insulated Panels: Increases sheeting weight and cost but required for cold storage.

Real-World Example

Consider a warehouse PEB with:

  • Length: 40 m
  • Width / Span: 20 m
  • Eave Height: 6 m
  • Bay Spacing: 6 m
  • No crane

Footprint = 40 × 20 = 800 m². Using a typical factor of 10–12 kg/m² for a standard warehouse, estimated steel weight would be around 8 to 10 tons. Sheeting area would include roof plus walls. This gives a quick budget figure before detailed design.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Forgetting to include crane load — this can increase steel weight by 30% or more.
  • Using only footprint area for walls — wall area requires perimeter × height.
  • Not accounting for insulated panels when the building is for cold storage.
  • Using feet and meters mixed without proper conversion.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Is this calculator accurate enough for ordering steel?
A: No, it's for preliminary estimates. Final ordering requires detailed structural design.

Q: Can I use it for cold storage PEB?
A: Yes, check the insulated panel option and combine with our Cold Storage Calculator.

Q: Does it include foundation?
A: No, it estimates steel and sheeting only. Foundation design is separate.
