❄️ What Is ColdMatrix?

ColdMatrix is a digital tools platform that provides free, practical online calculators and engineering utilities for engineers, contractors, builders, business owners, technicians, and general users. Our tools simplify calculations for cold storage, sandwich panels, prefab construction, steel structures, material estimation, and everyday business needs.

But ColdMatrix is more than just a calculator website. It is directly connected to Cold Matrix Advanced Solutions — our professional engineering and construction business. This connection ensures every tool we build is rooted in real‑world project experience, not just theory.

🏗️ Cold Matrix Advanced Solutions

Cold Matrix Advanced Solutions is our professional engineering and construction business. We specialise in:

  • PU/PIR Sandwich Panels — supply, installation, and fabrication
  • Cold Storage & Cold Rooms — complete construction, fabrication, and installation
  • Refrigeration Systems & Solutions — design, supply, and installation
  • Cold Store Doors & Accessories — insulated doors, hinges, locks, gaskets
  • Prefabricated Buildings & Warehouses — industrial and commercial prefab construction
  • LGS (Light Gauge Steel) & PEB (Pre‑Engineered Building) Structures
  • Industrial Sheds & Structural Solutions
  • Residential & Commercial Panel Blocks (offices, control rooms, living spaces)
  • Refrigeration Vans for food & meat transport
  • Portable Cabins & Site Offices
  • Complete installation services for cold stores & PEB buildings

We work with reliable international brands for compressors and refrigeration equipment, including Bitzer (Germany), Copeland, Frascold, and Gianni condensing units, evaporators, and accessories.
