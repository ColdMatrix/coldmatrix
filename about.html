ColdMatrix Solutions & Services is a division of Saleem & Sons Enterprise, guided by the legacy of our late father, Muhammad Saleem Arain (Rest in Peace), whose hard work and vision continue to inspire everything we do. Founded in 2018, we have successfully completed projects across Pakistan and the UAE, earning a reputation for reliability, quality, and honest engineering.

🎯 Our Mission

"To deliver uncompromising quality and reliable construction in cold storage, prefab buildings, and sandwich panel solutions — and to empower the industry with free, experience‑based engineering tools."

ColdMatrix Tools was not built by theorists. It was built by a cold‑storage contractor with over 10 years of hands‑on work. Every calculator, every recommendation, comes from real projects, real measurements, and real mistakes corrected over a decade in the field.

🏗️ Our Core Services

  • PEB Structures & Prefab Constructions
  • Cold Storage & Freezer Rooms – design, supply & installation
  • PU/PIR Sandwich Panels – manufacturing, supply & installation
  • Residential & Commercial Panel Blocks (offices, control rooms, living spaces)
  • Portable Cabins & Site Offices
  • Refrigeration Vans for food & meat transport
  • Complete installation services for cold stores & PEB buildings

🏭 Local Manufacturing – Karachi, Pakistan

We now manufacture import‑quality PU/PIR sandwich panels right here in Karachi, in collaboration with a trusted international partner. These panels meet the same standards we once imported from Dubai (Kingspan, TSSC, and Emirates Industrial Panels) — but produced locally to serve you faster and more affordably.

🧠 Experience You Can Trust

With 10+ years of practical, on‑site cold storage experience, we have worked directly with world‑leading brands – Bitzer (Germany), Copeland, Frascold, and reliable Gianni condensing units, evaporators, and accessories. Before local manufacturing, we spent three years importing premium panels, which gave us deep knowledge of international quality and thermal performance.
