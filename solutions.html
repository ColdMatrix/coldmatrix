🏆 Over 20 Years of Industrial Legacy

Engineering the Cold Chain,
Building the Future

Born from a deep understanding of thermal insulation and prefabricated structures, Cold Matrix Advanced Solutions is the industrial arm behind the tools you trust. We design, supply, install, and maintain complete cold storage facilities, sandwich panel systems, and prefab buildings — all under one roof.

📜 Our Roots

The journey began in 2018 with our parent company, Slim & Sense, which established a reputation for importing premium sandwich panels from the Gulf and Europe. In 2024, ColdMatrix Tools was launched to bring industrial‑grade calculators to engineers worldwide. Today, Cold Matrix Advanced Solutions merges that 20‑year legacy of hands‑on construction experience with modern digital precision — offering a truly integrated service from calculation to completion.

🔧 Turnkey Industrial Services

  • Cold Storage & Freezer Rooms — Design, manufacturing, and installation of chiller rooms (+25°C to 0°C), freezer rooms (0°C to -20°C), deep freezers (-25°C), and blast freezers / blood bank storage down to -50°C. We use imported refrigeration units from Bitzer, Copeland, Frascold and more, configured to your exact load requirements.
  • PU/PIR Sandwich Panels — Supply and installation of roof, wall, and partition panels. We import from world‑leading brands like Kingspan, DSSC, Emirates Industrial Panels and many others. Available in flat, louvre, corrugated, and concealed‑fix profiles.
  • Prefab Construction — Complete prefabricated buildings: office blocks, residential homes, penthouses, marquee / banquet halls, and site accommodation. Fast, durable, and custom‑designed.
  • Portable Cabins & Refrigerated Vans — Portable site cabins, security booths, and small refrigerated containers. We also build insulated refrigerated vans for temperature‑sensitive transport.
  • Professional Installation Teams — Dedicated crews for panel erection, cold room assembly, refrigeration piping, and electrical integration. We handle everything from the floor slab up.
