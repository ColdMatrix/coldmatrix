📜 Our Roots

The journey began in 2018 with our parent company, Saleem & Sons Enterprises, which quickly established a reputation for importing premium sandwich panels from the Gulf and Europe. Over the years, we expanded into cold storage design, refrigeration, and prefab construction. In 2024, Cold Matrix Advanced Solutions was formally launched to bring industrial‑grade engineering and manufacturing under one roof. Today, we merge more than a decade of hands‑on construction experience with modern digital precision — offering a truly integrated service from calculation to completion.

🔧 Turnkey Industrial Services

  • Cold Storage & Freezer Rooms — Design, manufacturing, and installation of chiller rooms (+25°C to 0°C), freezer rooms (0°C to -20°C), deep freezers (-25°C), and blast freezers / blood bank storage down to -50°C. We use imported refrigeration units from Bitzer, Copeland, Frascold and more, configured to your exact load requirements.
  • PU/PIR Sandwich Panels — Supply and installation of roof, wall, and partition panels. We import from world‑leading brands like Kingspan, TSSC, Emirates Industrial Panels and many others. Available in flat, louvre, corrugated, and concealed‑fix profiles.
  • Prefab Construction — Complete prefabricated buildings: office blocks, residential homes, penthouses, marquee / banquet halls, and site accommodation. We specialize in Light Gauge Steel (LGS) and Pre‑Engineered Building (PEB) structures for faster, lighter, and more flexible construction.
  • Portable Cabins & Refrigerated Vans — Portable site cabins, security booths, and small refrigerated containers. We also build insulated refrigerated vans for temperature‑sensitive transport.
  • Professional Installation Teams — Dedicated crews for panel erection, cold room assembly, refrigeration piping, and electrical integration. We handle everything from the floor slab up.

🆕 Specialized Cold Room Types

  • Dock Rooms / Transit Rooms — Temperature-controlled loading/unloading areas that maintain the cold chain during logistics. We install dock shelters, air curtains, and insulated doors to minimize heat ingress and protect product integrity.
  • Multipurpose Cold Storage — Flexible multi-temperature zones within one facility. Independent evaporators and control systems allow you to store different products (fruits, meat, dairy, pharma) under one roof at different set points.
  • Blast Freezers & Rapid Chill Systems — High-airflow systems designed to rapidly bring product temperatures down to -40°C, preserving quality and extending shelf life. Ideal for food processing, seafood, and export facilities.
  • Large Industrial Cold Stores — Multi-story cold storage warehouses, distribution centers, and controlled atmosphere storage with racking, mezzanines, and advanced refrigeration control.

🧱 Insulation & Roofing Materials

We are a leading supplier of insulation products for industrial and commercial projects. Our range includes:

  • PU/PIR Sandwich Panels — 50mm to 200mm thickness, factory‑direct or imported premium brands. Suitable for cold rooms, clean rooms, and building envelopes.
  • EPS & XPS Boards — Expanded and extruded polystyrene insulation boards. Available in various densities and thicknesses, including 3‑inch XPS for floor insulation, roof insulation, and cavity walls.
  • PUF Board Sheets — Prefabricated polyurethane foam boards for roof, wall, and floor insulation. Lightweight, high R‑value, and easy to install under concrete slabs or metal roofs.
  • PVC Imported Roofing Sheets — Durable, weather‑resistant roofing sheets in multiple profiles and colors. Ideal for industrial, agricultural, and commercial buildings where corrosion resistance and longevity are required.
  • Complete Roof Installation — From simple sheet fixing to full insulated roof systems with purlins, flashings, and insulation. We provide turnkey roofing solutions for any structure.

🏗️ Structural & Modular Solutions

  • Light Gauge Steel (LGS) Structures — Engineered light steel framing for faster, lighter, and more flexible construction. Suitable for residential villas, schools, clinics, and multi‑story buildings. Excellent seismic resistance and thermal performance.
  • Large Halls & Market Roofs — Clear‑span structures for markets, exhibition halls, factories, and storage facilities. We specialize in banker's market and shopping mall roofs with specialized roof sections.
  • Complete PEB (Pre‑Engineered Buildings) — Turnkey PEB steel structures for warehouses, factories, aircraft hangars, and industrial plants. We handle design, fabrication, and erection, with clear spans up to 80 feet and beyond.
  • Penthouses & Residential Extensions — Prefabricated penthouses and room additions that are fast to install, energy‑efficient, and fully customizable.
