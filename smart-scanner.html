About the Smart Scanner

ColdMatrix Smart Scanner combines QR code and barcode scanning, a QR code generator, a barcode generator, and OCR text extraction in one elegant tool. Scan live with your camera, upload images, generate codes, and extract text — all privately in your browser with no uploads, no accounts, and no tracking.

Built for global users, this tool works entirely offline after the first load and respects your privacy. Whether you’re sharing a website, tracking inventory, or digitizing printed text, the Smart Scanner is fast, reliable, and always free.

Common Questions

Is my data uploaded? No — all scanning and OCR processing happens locally on your device.

Can I scan barcodes? Yes, the camera and upload scanner support common 1D and 2D barcode formats.
