📖 Complete Guide to Smart Scanner

The ColdMatrix Smart Scanner is a powerful, all-in-one tool that lets you scan QR codes, barcodes, and extract text from images using your device's camera or uploaded files. It also includes built-in generators for QR codes and barcodes, making it a complete scanning and generation toolkit. The tool runs entirely in your browser — no uploads, no registration, and no tracking. Your data stays private on your device.

What Exactly Is a Smart Scanner?

A smart scanner is a digital utility that uses your camera or image files to detect and decode machine-readable codes such as QR codes and barcodes. It can also perform Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to extract printed or handwritten text from images. The ColdMatrix Smart Scanner goes a step further by integrating code generators, allowing you to create your own QR codes and barcodes on the fly.

This makes it ideal for a wide range of tasks — from quickly sharing a website link to tracking inventory, scanning product information, or digitising documents. Because all processing happens locally, it's safe to use even with sensitive business or personal data.

Key Features of ColdMatrix Smart Scanner

  • Live Camera Scan: Use your device camera to scan QR codes and barcodes in real time.
  • Image Upload Scan: Upload an image containing a QR code or barcode to decode it.
  • QR Code Generator: Create custom QR codes from any text or URL with adjustable styling.
  • Barcode Generator: Generate barcodes in EAN-13, UPC-A, Code 128, and Code 39 formats.
  • OCR Text Extraction: Extract text from images using Tesseract.js, including multi-language support.
  • Privacy First: All scanning, generation, and OCR processing happens locally in your browser.
  • No Accounts or Fees: The tool is completely free with no hidden charges or sign-ups.

How to Use the Smart Scanner

  1. Choose a tab: Select the function you need — Live Scan, Upload, QR Generator, Barcode Generator, or Text OCR.
  2. For scanning: Allow camera access for live scan, or upload an image. The result appears instantly in the result box.
  3. For QR generation: Enter your text or URL, click "Generate QR Code", and the code will appear. You can right-click to save it.
  4. For barcode generation: Enter the barcode number, select the type, and click "Generate Barcode". The barcode SVG is displayed.
  5. For OCR: Upload an image, wait for the progress indicator, and the extracted text will appear in the result box.
  6. Reset any time: Use the "Reset All" button to clear all outputs and return to the first tab.

Tips for Best Results

  • Ensure good lighting: For live scanning, hold the camera steady and make sure the code is well-lit and fully visible.
  • Focus on the code: Move the camera closer or further until the code is detected.
  • High contrast images: For OCR, use clear, high-resolution images with dark text on a light background.
  • Correct barcode format: Choose the right barcode type; EAN-13 and UPC require specific digit lengths.
  • Check your QR content: Always verify the URL or text before generating or sharing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data uploaded to a server?
No. All scanning, generation, and OCR processing is done locally in your browser using JavaScript libraries. Nothing is uploaded or stored externally.

Does the camera scan work on mobile?
Yes. The live scan uses the HTML5 QR Code library, which works on Android and iOS browsers. You must grant camera permission when prompted.

Can I scan barcodes with the live camera?
Yes, the camera scan supports common 1D and 2D barcode formats, including QR, EAN-13, UPC-A, Code 128, and more.

What image formats are supported for upload?
JPG, PNG, and most common image formats. For best results, use images with clear, well-defined codes or text.

Is OCR available for languages other than English?
Yes, Tesseract.js supports many languages, though English is the default. You can modify the code to specify additional language packs.
