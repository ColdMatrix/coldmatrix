📖 The Complete Executive Business Planner Guide

The ColdMatrix Business Planner is a premium executive digital diary — proudly invented and created by the ColdMatrix Business Team for business owners, entrepreneurs, CEOs, managers, and professionals worldwide. It brings together all essential business planning tools in one beautiful, paper-style interface that works on phone, tablet, and desktop.

🎯 Complete Section Guide

📊 Summary Dashboard: Your weekly overview at a glance. See total counts for every category — goals, meetings, tasks, trips, marketing plans, expenses, contacts, and notes. The "Upcoming This Week" section automatically shows meetings, task deadlines, and trips scheduled within the next 7 days, so you never miss anything important.

🎯 Goals & Targets: Set clear business objectives with three time horizons — Quarterly (3 months), Half-Yearly (6 months), and Yearly (12 months). Each goal includes a target date and notes field, helping you stay focused on what truly matters for business growth.

📅 Important Meetings: Schedule all your business meetings with complete details — meeting title, date & time, attendee name, and agenda. Never forget a client presentation or team discussion again. Perfect for busy professionals managing multiple stakeholders.

✅ Business To-Do Tasks: Manage daily business tasks with priority levels — High (🔴), Medium (🟡), or Low (🟢). Set due dates to ensure critical tasks are completed on time. Mark tasks as done with a single tap and watch them get struck through.

✈️ Business Trips: Plan all your business travel in advance. Record destination, departure date, return date, and trip purpose. Whether it's a client visit, trade show, or regional meeting — your travel plans are organized in one place.

📈 Marketing Plans: Track your marketing campaigns with budget allocation, start dates, and detailed notes. Monitor which campaigns are active, planned, or completed. Essential for small business owners managing their own marketing.

💰 Business Expenses: Record every business expense with amount, category (Office, Travel, Marketing, Utilities, Salaries, Other), and date. Maintain financial control and understand where your money goes. Perfect for budgeting and tax preparation.

👥 Business Contacts: Build your private business contact directory. Store names, companies, phone numbers, email addresses, and notes. Your own personal CRM — no external service needed.

📝 Business Notes: A free-form writing area for ideas, meeting notes, supplier information, or any important business information. Your digital notebook within the planner.

💾 Backup, Export & Import — Your Safety Net

Because all data lives only in your browser, we've added two crucial safety features on the Summary page:

  • Export Backup (.json): Downloads every entry across all 8 sections — goals, meetings, tasks, trips, marketing, expenses, contacts, notes — as a single file. Save it to your phone, email, or cloud drive.
  • Import Backup: Restores your data from any backup file. Works perfectly on a new phone, after a browser reset, or if you simply want to move your planner. Imported entries are merged safely with your existing data — nothing is overwritten.
  • Export as PDF / JPG: For visual snapshots of your weekly summary — perfect for printing or sharing with your team.

We recommend exporting a backup every week if your planner holds important business information.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is my data safe?
A: Yes, 100% safe. All data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. Nothing is ever uploaded to a server.

Q: Can I use this offline?
A: Yes! After first load, the planner works completely offline.

Q: Will my data be lost if I close the browser?
A: No. Your data persists automatically. Just open the planner again and everything is there.

Q: How do I back up my planner data?
A: Go to the Summary tab and click Export Backup (.json). A single file with all your data downloads.

Q: Can I restore my data on a new phone?
A: Yes. Open the planner on your new device, go to Summary, click Import Backup, and select your .json file. Everything is restored safely.

Q: Will importing overwrite my existing entries?
A: Never. Imported items are merged with what you already have — nothing is deleted or overwritten.

Q: Can I export my planner as PDF or image?
A: Yes — the Summary page has Export as PDF and Export as JPG buttons for your weekly overview.

Q: Is this really free forever?
A: Yes, it's 100% free. Made by ColdMatrix for business users worldwide.
