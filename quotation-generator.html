📖 Complete Guide to Quotation Generator

A quotation generator is a digital tool that helps businesses and freelancers create professional, itemised price quotes quickly and accurately. Instead of writing quotes by hand or using complex software, you simply enter your business details, client information, and line items, and the tool automatically calculates totals, taxes, and currency conversions. The result is a polished document ready to share or print.

What Exactly Is a Quotation Generator?

A quotation generator (also called a quote maker or estimate builder) is a specialised calculator that assembles a formal pricing document. It typically includes fields for your company name, contact information, client details, a list of products or services with quantities and rates, and any applicable taxes. The tool then calculates the subtotal, tax, and grand total, and presents the information in a clean, professional layout.

Unlike a simple invoice maker, a quotation generator focuses on the pre‑sale stage—helping you communicate your proposed prices to a potential customer. Once the customer accepts, the quotation can often be converted into an invoice.

Why Use a Quotation Generator?

Tips for Creating Professional Quotations

  • Always use a clear quotation number for tracking and reference.
  • Add a validity period to encourage prompt decisions.
  • Itemise everything — transparency builds trust.
  • Mention payment terms (e.g., 50% advance, balance on delivery).
  • Double‑check quantities and rates before sending.
  • Include your contact details so the client can reach you easily.
  • Use a professional tone and proofread your notes.
