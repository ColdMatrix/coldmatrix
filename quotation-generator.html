📖 Quotation & Invoice Maker — Complete Business Guide

This dual-function tool helps businesses create professional quotations and GST invoices in under 60 seconds. A quotation is a pre-sale document showing proposed prices. An invoice is a post-sale document requesting payment. Both include line items, quantities, rates, taxes, discounts, and totals — all calculated automatically as you type.

What Is a Quotation?

A quotation (also called quote, estimate, or bid) is a formal document listing proposed prices for products or services. It includes your business details, client information, itemised products with quantities and rates, and the total amount. Quotations typically have a validity period (e.g., 15 days) and become binding once accepted by the customer.

What Is an Invoice?

An invoice is a commercial document requesting payment for goods or services already delivered. It includes invoice number, date, line items, subtotal, discount, tax/GST/VAT, and grand total. Invoices are essential for accounting, tax filing, and payment tracking.

Difference Between Quotation and Invoice

  • Timing: Quotation = before sale. Invoice = after sale.
  • Purpose: Quotation = propose price. Invoice = request payment.
  • Binding: Quotation = not binding until accepted. Invoice = legally binding on both parties.
  • Validity: Quotations expire. Invoices do not (but may have payment due dates).

Tips for Professional Documents

  • Always use a unique document number for tracking (e.g., Q-250912-001).
  • Add a validity period to encourage prompt decisions.
  • Itemise everything for transparency — avoid lump sums.
  • Mention payment terms clearly (advance %, due date).
  • Double-check quantities and rates before sending.
  • Save a copy of every document you send — our Save button stores it locally.
