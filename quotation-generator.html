📖 Quotation & Invoice Maker — Complete Business Guide

This dual-function tool helps businesses create professional quotations (quotes) and GST invoices in minutes. A quotation is a pre-sale document showing proposed prices. An invoice is a post-sale document requesting payment. Both include line items, quantities, rates, taxes, discounts, and totals — all calculated automatically as you type.

What Is a Quotation?

A quotation (also called quote, estimate, or bid) is a formal document listing proposed prices for products or services. It includes your business details, client information, itemised products with quantities and rates, and the total amount. Quotations typically have a validity period (e.g., 15 days) and become binding once accepted by the customer.

What Is an Invoice?

An invoice is a commercial document requesting payment for goods or services already delivered. It includes invoice number, date, line items, subtotal, discount, tax/GST/VAT, and grand total. Invoices are essential for accounting, tax filing, and payment tracking.

Tips for Professional Documents

  • Always use a unique document number for tracking.
  • Add a validity period to encourage prompt decisions.
  • Itemise everything for transparency.
  • Mention payment terms clearly.
  • Double-check quantities and rates before sending.
