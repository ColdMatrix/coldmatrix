About the Sandwich Panel Weight & Price Calculator

Use this tool to calculate PUF and PIR sandwich panel weight, quantity, and cost for roof panels, wall panels, and cold room panels. It's built for prefab construction and cold storage projects, using standard panel widths (roof panels 970mm, wall panels 1190mm) taken at full structural span with no artificial cuts — matching how panels are actually ordered and installed on site.

Common Questions

What thickness of sandwich panel do I need? Thickness depends on the required insulation (R-value) for your application — cold rooms typically need thicker panels (100-150mm) than general cladding (40-80mm). This calculator lets you select thickness and see weight/cost update instantly.

PUF panel vs PIR panel — which is cheaper? PUF (polyurethane) panels are generally more economical; PIR panels cost more but offer better fire performance, often required by building codes for commercial cold storage.
