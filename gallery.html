❄️ Cold Storage Projects

Our cold storage facilities range from small +4°C fruit and vegetable storage to deep -25°C frozen warehouses and blast freezers at -35°C. Each project is engineered to match:

  • Product sensitivity — dairy, meat, seafood, pharma, fruits, vegetables
  • Temperature profile — from +15°C ripening rooms down to -40°C blast freezing
  • Humidity control — critical for produce, cheese, and chocolate storage
  • Turnover rate — determines door type, racking layout, and cooling capacity
  • GMP / HACCP compliance — for pharmaceutical and food-grade requirements

We use PUF (Polyurethane Foam) and PIR (Polyisocyanurate) panels in thicknesses from 50mm to 200mm, based on the temperature zone. Doors are typically heavy-duty sliding or hinged type with heaters to prevent condensation and frost lock.

🧱 Sandwich Panel Manufacturing

Our in-house manufacturing produces high-quality insulated sandwich panels with:

  • PUF core — density 40–42 kg/m³, best for cold storage
  • PIR core — density 40–45 kg/m³, better fire performance
  • Steel skins — pre-painted galvanized steel (PPGI), 0.4–0.6 mm thick
  • Panel widths — 1,000 mm standard, custom sizes available
  • Lengths — up to 12 meters (transport limitation)

Panels are produced on a continuous lamination line, ensuring uniform density, consistent thickness, and precise interlocking edges (tongue-and-groove). Every batch passes thermal conductivity and density testing before dispatch.

🏠 Insulated Roofing Solutions

Insulated roofing panels provide a single-layer solution that eliminates the need for traditional insulation layers. They keep buildings 6–8°C cooler than uninsulated roofs, dramatically reducing HVAC costs. Common applications include:

  • Factory and warehouse roofs
  • PEB (Pre-Engineered Building) roof and wall cladding
  • Poultry and livestock sheds
  • Cold storage roofs
  • Agricultural storage buildings

Available in corrugated, standing seam, and micro-ribbed profiles, in colors from grey to RAL-custom finishes.
