❄️ Cold Storage Projects

Our cold storage facilities range from small +4°C fruit and vegetable storage to deep -25°C frozen warehouses. Each project is engineered to match product sensitivity, temperature profile, humidity control, and GMP/HACCP compliance requirements. We use PUF and PIR sandwich panels in thicknesses from 50mm to 200mm based on the temperature zone.

🧱 Sandwich Panel Supply

Our panels are manufactured with PUF core (density 40–42 kg/m³) or PIR core (density 40–45 kg/m³), pre-painted galvanized steel skins (0.4–0.6 mm), standard widths of 1,000–1,190 mm, and lengths up to 12 meters. Every batch passes thermal conductivity and density testing before dispatch.

🏗️ PEB Warehouse Structures

We deliver turnkey PEB warehouses with clear spans up to 80 feet, using ASTM A36, A572, and equivalent structural steel. Our scope covers foundation, steel erection, roof and wall sheeting, and complete handover for industrial logistics operations.
