Choosing the Right Panel Thickness for Your Cold Room

Published by ColdMatrix • 5 min read

One of the most common questions we get is: "What thickness of sandwich panel do I need for my cold room?" The answer depends on your target temperature, ambient conditions, and budget. Here's the definitive guide.

Quick Reference Table

  • +10°C to +20°C (Cool Room): 50mm panels
  • 0°C to +10°C (Chiller): 60-75mm panels
  • -5°C to 0°C (Cold Room): 100mm panels
  • -18°C to -5°C (Freezer): 120mm panels
  • -25°C to -18°C (Deep Freezer): 150mm panels
  • Below -25°C (Blast Freezer): 200mm panels

Why Thickness Matters

Panel thickness directly affects the U-value (thermal transmittance). A lower U-value means better insulation. For example, a 50mm PUF panel has a U-value of approximately 0.44 W/m²·K, while a 120mm panel drops to 0.18 W/m²·K — more than twice as effective.

Cost vs Performance

Thicker panels cost more upfront but save significantly on electricity over the life of the cold room. For a -18°C freezer in Karachi's 45°C summer, upgrading from 100mm to 120mm can reduce your electricity bill by 15-20% annually.

Our Recommendation

Always go one step thicker than the minimum requirement. The extra insulation pays for itself within 2-3 years through energy savings alone.
