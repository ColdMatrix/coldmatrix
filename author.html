ColdMatrix Engineering Team

Cold Matrix Advanced Solutions is a Karachi-based engineering firm continuing the legacy of its founder, the late Muhammad Saleem Arain.

With over 8 years of hands-on field experience across Pakistan and the Middle East, our team specializes in:

  • Cold storage facilities and industrial refrigeration
  • PU/PIR sandwich panel manufacturing and installation
  • PEB (Pre-Engineered Building) steel structures
  • Turnkey prefab construction and insulation projects

Every tool on ColdMatrix Tools is built on real project experience — not generic theory. Our calculators reflect the challenges, formulas, and standards we've applied on actual job sites from Karachi to Balochistan.
