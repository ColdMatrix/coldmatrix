🌱 About Waseem

Waseem Akhtar is the Founder and CEO of Cold Matrix Advanced Solutions — a Karachi-based engineering firm specializing in cold storage construction, PU/PIR sandwich panel buildings, PEB steel structures, and prefabricated industrial construction across Pakistan.

With 9+ years of hands-on field experience, Waseem has personally overseen the design, fabrication, and installation of cold rooms, freezer facilities, PEB warehouses, and insulated panel structures for clients in the food, poultry, pharmaceutical, and logistics sectors.

His work is grounded in practical reality — not textbooks. Every calculator and guide on ColdMatrix Tools is shaped by the challenges, formulas, and standards he has applied on actual job sites from Karachi to Jamshoro to Balochistan.
