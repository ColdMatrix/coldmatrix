PEB vs Traditional Construction — Full Cost Comparison

Published by ColdMatrix • 5 min read

When planning a warehouse, factory, or cold storage facility, one of the first decisions is: Pre-Engineered Building (PEB) or traditional brick-and-mortar? Here's the complete comparison based on real projects in Pakistan.

Construction Time

  • PEB with sandwich panels: 4-8 weeks for a 10,000 sq ft facility
  • Traditional RCC: 4-6 months minimum

Cost Comparison (per sq ft)

  • PEB structure + sandwich panels: PKR 1,800-2,500
  • Traditional RCC + brickwork: PKR 2,200-3,000

Additional PEB Advantages

  • 70% faster construction
  • Better thermal insulation (6-8°C cooler)
  • Easy to expand or relocate
  • Lower foundation costs (lighter structure)
  • 30%+ energy savings over building lifetime

Conclusion

For industrial and commercial buildings, PEB with insulated sandwich panels is the clear winner — cheaper upfront, faster to build, and significantly cheaper to operate.
