About the Notepad

Write and organize notes right in your browser — meeting notes, ideas, shopping lists, or anything else. Every note auto-saves as you type, so you never lose your work, and you can search across all your notes instantly or download any note as a plain text file.

Common Questions

Are my notes uploaded anywhere? No — everything is stored locally in your own browser only. Nothing is ever sent to a server, so your notes stay completely private to your device.

Will I lose my notes if I close the tab? No — your notes are saved automatically and will still be there next time you visit, as long as you use the same browser and device and don't clear your browser data.
