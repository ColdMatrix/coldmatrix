About the ColdMatrix Cold Store & Sandwich Panel Calculator

This free tool helps engineers, contractors, and cold storage builders quickly size a cold room and estimate PUF (polyurethane foam) and PIR sandwich panel requirements — covering roof panels, wall panels, floor panels, insulation thickness, thermal heat load, refrigerant selection, and estimated construction cost.

Common Questions

What's the difference between PUF and PIR sandwich panels? PUF panels are the most common and cost-effective insulation for cold rooms. PIR panels offer better fire resistance, often required by fire safety codes.

Does this work for PEB / steel structure cold storage? Yes — pair it with our PEB Material Calculator and Steel Weight Calculator.
